Why this session exists

Public Act 104-0657

“Wireless communication device” does not include a device issued by the school or a teacher for educational purposes.

SB 2427 · signed July 28, 2026 · effective 2027–28

The statute regulates one channel of the screen ecology and exempts the rest by definition. Every Illinois district will be compliant by adopting the ISBE template. None will know, on that basis alone, whether anything about learning changed.

ISBE said as much in its own model policy: teachers estimate one in three students are using laptops at school for nonacademic purposes. The state named the second problem in the same document that directs districts to solve the first.

Bring one thing

Before October 27
  • Your draft wireless communication device policy — or the ISBE Model Cell Phone Policy released in August 2026. Either works, and the session is built so that arriving with the template alone costs you nothing.
  • The name of your approved AI or writing platform. One segment asks what it does by default when a student opens it. Most districts have never checked.
  • Check which clock you are on. Districts with a qualifying policy in place before July 28, 2026 may hold it through 2030–31 rather than conforming by 2027–28.
